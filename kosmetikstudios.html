Knowledge & Guide

Personalized cosmetics & private label for your beauty studio — the complete overview.

Why your own skincare line elevates your studio

An own product line is more than just an upsell for a beauty studio. It creates recognition, builds client loyalty and positions you as an expert. With personalized cosmetics under your own private label, you give your clients skincare products to take home that they can only get from you. This extends the effect of your treatment far beyond the studio visit and creates a recurring revenue stream. Every cream and every serum with your name becomes a quiet brand ambassador that reminds your clients of your studio every day.

Skin analysis as the basis for individual care

Standard skincare rarely fits every skin. With a skin analysis you can see what your clients' skin actually needs — and respond to it precisely. Novogenia provides the scientific basis from our own lab. From the results comes personalized cosmetics matched exactly to each skin type. This sets your studio apart from the competition and makes your advice noticeably more valuable. Once someone has experienced how much better care tailored to their own skin works, they stay loyal to your studio.

White-label skincare: your brand, not ours

Your clients should associate your studio with high-quality care — not an unfamiliar manufacturer. That's why we produce all products as white-label skincare: creams, serums and skincare products appear under your logo, your label and your packaging. Novogenia stays in the background as the producer. This creates a true private label with which you strengthen your beauty studio over the long term, instead of promoting someone else's brands. Your clients aren't buying just any product — they're buying your product, and that lastingly strengthens their bond with your studio.

Three ways to your own cosmetics line

You choose how to get started. First: finished products to resell — proven cosmetics, ready to use with no lead time. Second: white-label under your brand — the same high-quality products with your branding. Third: your own product idea — you bring the concept or desired formulation, we develop and produce it. All three paths start with a no-obligation conversation and can be tailored to your studio. You can start with a few products and gradually expand your skincare line into a complete range.

Starting from small batch — begin without inventory risk

High minimum quantities are often an obstacle for a studio and tie up capital unnecessarily. Thanks to modern technology, Novogenia produces from small batch — ideal for beauty studios that want to test first. You start with a small batch, gain experience with your clients, and only scale up once demand is there. That keeps your inventory lean and significantly lowers the risk of building your private label. So you stay flexible and can adjust your range to your clients' wishes at any time.

Made in Austria — quality that wins clients over

Production in Austria stands for strict EU requirements, tested raw materials and full traceability. Made in Austria is therefore a strong selling point with your clients, especially in the premium cosmetics segment. Novogenia has been developing and producing in its own certified lab in Salzburg since 2009. This connection of science and production makes us a reliable partner for your personalized cosmetics. Short distances and in-house production mean more control, consistent quality and fair prices without middlemen.

More value for your clients — and your studio

Your own line of personalized cosmetics makes your beauty studio not only more unique but also more attractive commercially. You no longer just sell treatments — you offer a complete skincare experience of skin analysis, advice and matching products for home. This increases the value of every appointment, improves your clients' visible results and creates a recurring revenue stream through repeat purchases. A true private label clearly sets your studio apart from competitors who only resell someone else's branded products off the shelf.

How to find the right manufacturer

Look for three things: an in-house lab instead of outsourced production, the ability to perform a genuine skin analysis, and a start from small batch under your own brand. Novogenia offers all three under one roof.
