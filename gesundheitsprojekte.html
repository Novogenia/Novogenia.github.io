Knowledge & Guide

Genetic analyses for health projects — the complete overview.

Genetic analyses in a public-health context

Genetic information is gaining importance for public health — for example in prevention programs, in nutrition and lifestyle counseling or in large-scale studies. It can help make individual needs visible and target preventive measures more precisely.

State health projects need a partner laboratory that can reliably handle not just individual analyses but entire programs — over months and at consistent quality. What is required is predictable capacity, standardized workflows and a transparent, documented way of working. Novogenia has been active as a genetic lab since 2009 and operates its own certified EU lab in Salzburg, designed for scalable workflows and standardized processes as public programs require.

What a scalable partner laboratory must deliver

Unlike with individual orders, state health projects are about high sample numbers over longer periods. A suitable scalable partner laboratory must offer standardized processes, predictable capacity and end-to-end sample logistics — and maintain this quality even as volume grows.

Novogenia is designed for this: we handle genetic analyses as an outsourcing lab — from sample to structured report — and adapt capacity and workflows to the scope of your program. The partner having its own lab means short paths, clear responsibilities and full control over quality, even as participant numbers grow.

Screening and prevention programs

In prevention programs and screening initiatives, standardization and traceability are key. Novogenia aligns its workflows accordingly: uniform sample kits, defined shipping routes, regulated incoming inspection and structured report delivery.

This allows even large participant numbers to be handled in an orderly, traceable way — an important foundation for the quality and acceptance of a public health program. Reproducible, documented processes ensure that every sample is treated the same and that results remain comparable across the entire program lifetime. This creates the data basis needed to seriously assess the impact of preventive measures.

End-to-end sample logistics

The sample-to-report logistics are decisive in large programs. Novogenia supplies sample kits, organizes shipping and handles incoming inspection, analysis and report delivery. This closed, standardized process ensures traceable workflows across the entire project.

For the institutions involved this means considerable organizational relief: they need not build their own lab structures or take responsibility for complex logistics themselves, but instead receive a clearly regulated workflow with a single point of contact. Especially with many sites or participants, such end-to-end logistics are a decisive success factor. This keeps the program manageable even as its scope grows, and responsibilities are clearly defined across the entire duration.

Quality, EU standard and “Made in Austria”

Particularly in publicly accountable projects, quality and traceability are central. Novogenia analyzes in its own certified facility in Salzburg to EU standard and ensures documented, traceable workflows.

An analysis in Austria or the EU — Made in Austria — is a quality and trust argument that can matter for the acceptance of a state health project. Transparent processes and a clear point of contact also make coordination with the responsible bodies and answering follow-up questions easier across the entire project duration.

Joint project development & white-label

Public-health projects are rarely off the shelf. That is why we develop tailor-made concepts with you: specific analysis packages, matching report structures and, on request, white-label solutions in which analyses and reports appear under the branding of your project or institution.

This keeps public communication in your hands, while the lab processing rests with the experienced partner laboratory. In a structured process, we jointly clarify goals, target group and expected sample volume, and translate these requirements into a concrete, scalable program — from sample collection to report structure.

Personalized supplements within the program

Beyond pure analysis, Novogenia can on request produce personalized supplements as an own brand — for instance as part of a preventive care concept within your health project. Based on the analysis results, we manufacture individually tailored supplements, entirely under the branding of your project or institution.

Thanks to advanced galenics and in-house production in the EU lab, even larger quantities can be manufactured at consistent quality. Analysis and product thus combine into an end-to-end offering from a single source that can be flexibly integrated into the structure of a prevention or care program — with one single, accountable partner.
