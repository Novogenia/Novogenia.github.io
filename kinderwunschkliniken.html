Knowledge & Guide

Partner lab for fertility clinics — the complete overview.

Why a specialized partner laboratory?

Fertility and IVF clinics offer a broad range of services around family planning. Genetic analyses are an important building block here — but building them in-house is expensive, regulatorily demanding and ties up highly specialized staff.

A specialized partner laboratory takes over these analyses as an outsourcing lab, while the clinic concentrates on counseling and treatment. This lets you expand your range of genetic services without investing in your own lab infrastructure. High fixed costs for equipment, premises and staff become predictable costs per analysis. Novogenia has been active as a genetic lab since 2009 and operates its own certified EU lab in Salzburg — with reliable, discreet processes that are particularly important in the sensitive fertility field.

Genetic analyses around fertility

In the area of fertility, genetic analyses can provide valuable information. As a specialized outsourcing lab, we take over the analysis from sample to structured report — based on established procedures and under careful quality assurance.

Important here: we provide the laboratory service and deliver sound reports — the medical assessment, counseling and indication remain the responsibility of your clinic. This clear division of roles ensures that your physicians retain professional authority, while the lab processing rests with an experienced partner. Your clinic can thus credibly integrate the genetic offering into its existing counseling, without having to maintain lab capacity of its own.

Carrier screening as a laboratory service

Carrier screening is an established element of genetic diagnostics in the fertility field. Novogenia carries out such analyses as a contract laboratory service — handled end to end from our own EU lab, with traceable sample logistics and structured report delivery.

This way, your clinic receives reliable results without having to build lab capacity of its own. Standardized sample kits, defined shipping routes and a regulated incoming inspection ensure that every sample is correctly assigned and documented — a basic prerequisite for robust reports in the sensitive context of family planning. The entire process lies in the hands of a single, experienced partner laboratory with clear responsibilities.

White-label: your brand front and center

Particularly in the sensitive environment of a fertility clinic, a consistent, trustworthy presence is decisive. With a white-label solution, genetic analyses and reports appear under your clinic's name and layout.

Novogenia stays discreetly in the background as a partner laboratory, while the scientific and technical processing is carried out professionally and reliably. For your patients this creates a consistent, familiar brand experience — the reports fit seamlessly into your clinic's existing communication and strengthen trust in your institution.

Sample logistics and discretion

A key advantage of an experienced partner laboratory is its end-to-end sample logistics. Novogenia supplies sample kits, organizes shipping and handles incoming inspection, analysis and report delivery.

This closed, traceable process takes organizational load off your clinic and supports a discreet, orderly handling of sensitive samples. Because the entire workflow lies in the hands of a single partner laboratory, there are clear responsibilities and short paths for follow-up questions — an important factor for reliability and discretion in the fertility field. This lets your team concentrate on caring for patients while the lab processing runs reliably in the background.

Complementary personalized supplements

Beyond genetic analyses, Novogenia can on request produce personalized supplements as an own brand — for instance for complementary support as part of fertility care. Analysis and product thus combine into an end-to-end offering from a single source.

Thanks to advanced galenics such as slow-release and pellet technology, even demanding active-ingredient combinations can be implemented precisely. You can flexibly integrate such products into your clinic concept and offer them under your own brand — an additional building block that meaningfully complements your counseling, without you having to coordinate further suppliers.

Quality, EU standard and “Made in Austria”

Genetic analyses are subject to strict requirements in the EU. Novogenia analyzes in its own certified facility in Salzburg to EU standard and ensures documented, traceable workflows — from sample intake to the finished report.

Especially in the sensitive fertility field, this reliability is decisive. An analysis in Austria or the EU — Made in Austria — is not only a quality argument but also a trust argument toward your patients. Short paths to the lab and a clear point of contact also make internal coordination and answering follow-up questions easier.
