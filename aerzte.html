Knowledge & Guide

Genetic tests, blood analyses & personalized supplements in the medical practice — the complete overview.

Personalized medicine begins with the analysis

Individual recommendations need individual data. Genetic tests in the medical practice show how a patient utilizes nutrients and active substances — and thus create the basis for personalized advice. Novogenia provides these analyses from its own certified genetic lab. That lets you give your patients recommendations based on their genetic predisposition rather than on mere averages. This sets your practice apart and lastingly strengthens your patients' trust. Personalized medicine thus moves from an abstract concept to a concrete offering your patients experience directly in your practice.

Personalized supplements for your patients

After the analysis comes the implementation. With personalized supplements you give your patients exactly the supplementation that fits their individual needs. Novogenia produces these tailor-made blends in our own EU lab, matched to the analysis result. This creates a complete practice offering: analysis, recommendation and product from a single source, with no gaps and no detours. It makes your advice more valuable and creates clear added value for your patients. At the same time, it creates an additional revenue stream that keeps the value within your practice instead of handing it to external providers.

White-label lab: your practice at the center

Your patients trust your practice. That's why we offer all products as white-label: genetic tests and supplements appear under your logo, your label and your packaging. Novogenia acts as a white-label lab in the background and never appears to the outside. So your practice brand stays at the center while you draw on the infrastructure, approvals and know-how of an experienced genetic lab. You need neither your own lab equipment nor a team of geneticists — and still offer high-quality, personalized products under your name.

Three ways to start with Novogenia

You decide on the depth of the collaboration. First: practice supplies to resell — proven analyses and supplements, ready to use with no lead time. Second: white-label under your brand — the same products with your branding. Third: your own analysis idea — you bring the concept or composition, we develop and produce it. All three paths start with a no-obligation conversation and can be tailored to your practice. You don't have to commit from the start — you can begin small and expand your offering step by step as demand grows.

Starting from small batch — flexible practice supplies

Classic manufacturers often demand high minimum quantities and tie up capital unnecessarily. Thanks to modern technology, Novogenia produces from small batch — ideal for a medical practice that wants to test first. You start with a small batch, gain experience, and only scale up once demand is there. That makes practice supplies predictable and significantly lowers the commercial risk. So you can integrate new products into your daily practice with little risk and adjust your range to your patients' needs at any time.

Made in Austria — quality to EU standards

Production in Austria stands for strict EU requirements, tested raw materials and full traceability. Made in Austria is therefore a strong argument for trust with your patients. Novogenia has been developing and producing in its own certified genetic lab in Salzburg since 2009. This connection of science and production makes us a reliable lab partner for your medical practice. Short distances between analysis and manufacturing mean more control, consistent quality and fair prices, because no middlemen take a cut.

More value for your patients — and your practice

The combination of genetic tests, well-founded analysis and personalized supplements makes your medical practice a destination for individual health. You offer your patients a complete program of testing, advice and matching products — all under your practice brand and without the effort of running your own lab infrastructure. This strengthens your patients' trust, improves the quality of your advice and creates a recurring revenue stream through repeat orders. This noticeably differentiates your practice from providers who only recommend standard preparations.
