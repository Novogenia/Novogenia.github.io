Knowledge & Guide

Product partner for direct sales & MLM — the complete overview.

Why the right product is decisive in direct sales

In direct sales and network marketing, everything stands or falls with the product. An interchangeable range available everywhere undermines any sales story. An exclusive own-brand range of genetic tests, supplements and cosmetics, by contrast, delivers genuine talking points, differentiation and loyalty. Novogenia is the product partner that produces exactly this range in its own EU lab.

A strong, unique product also motivates your sales partners: those who stand behind the range sell with conviction. Personalized health products with a scientific background give your network exactly this credibility — and a reason why customers should buy from you in particular. This way the product itself becomes the strongest argument in the referral conversation and the foundation of a viable business build-up.

What does white label mean for direct sellers?

With the white-label solution you supply the idea or the desired range, and Novogenia produces the products under your logo, label and packaging. This creates your own, exclusive sales brand, without your own lab or production. Your partners and end customers see only your brand — the manufacturer stays in the background.

This way the brand belongs entirely to you and your sales system. You build your own brand value instead of advertising third-party products, and you secure the exclusivity that decides between success and interchangeability in network marketing. A range available solely through your network also protects you from price comparisons and competition over the same products.

Genetic tests as a story-rich premium product

A genetic test is an exceptionally strong product in referral marketing: it is personal, requires explanation and creates genuine advisory moments — exactly the environment in which direct sales work. The test naturally leads to the matching personalized supplements, and with it a range pathway of follow-up and repeat purchases.

This depth of explanation is an advantage in direct sales, not an obstacle: it creates the personal conversation starter that referral marketing thrives on. A DNA test opens the door, the personalized follow-up products generate recurring revenue — an ideal combination for a viable compensation model. This way you don't just win one-off buyers but build a loyal customer base that sustains your network over the long term.

Supplements and cosmetics from the EU lab

Novogenia produces supplements in many dosage forms — capsules, tablets, powders, sachets and, on request, slow-release or pellet technology — as well as personalized cosmetics. Everything is made in our own certified EU lab in Salzburg: Made in Austria, tested to EU standards. This tested quality is a strong trust argument for your sales partners and end customers.

In direct sales in particular, where personal recommendation is central, verifiable quality counts double. Our own EU lab, tested raw materials and complete traceability give your network the confidence of representing a truly high-quality product. This credibility transfers directly to your brand and strengthens the trust between your partners and their customers. In the referral business, where personal word-of-mouth is everything, tested quality thus becomes a genuine growth driver for your entire network.

Exclusive and scalable with your network

An exclusive range is a real competitive advantage in MLM — products available only through your network. Novogenia produces this range scalably: from the first batch for the build-up to large-scale production as your sales network grows. This way you remain available at all times without having to change partners.

Availability is decisive in direct sales: nothing slows a growing network like sold-out products. With production that grows alongside in our own EU lab, you ensure your range stays reliably available even during rapid growth — and your partners can focus fully on selling instead of on shortages.

Three ways to your sales range

You choose your model: Novogenia as a white-label manufacturer for your exclusive own brand, your own product idea that we realise, or proven Novogenia products as finished goods to resell. This way the concept fits every sales system — from the young network to the established direct-sales structure.

Young networks often start with finished products and switch to an exclusive own brand as they grow. We advise you on which route fits your structure, your compensation model and your growth goal — and accompany you in the transition from reselling to your own, exclusive sales brand.

Novogenia has been a genetic lab and manufacturer of personalized health products with its own lab in Salzburg since 2009. The combination of science and production creates a rare advantage: an exclusive, scalable range of genetic tests, supplements and cosmetics — as your own brand, in premium quality and with a genuine story.
