Knowledge & Guide

Genetics & micronutrients in physiotherapy — the complete overview.

Why micronutrients belong in physiotherapy

Physiotherapy doesn't end at the treatment table. Mobility, recovery and general well-being also depend on the individual supply of micronutrients. With personalized micronutrient products you give your patients a tool that sensibly complements the therapy and keeps working beyond the appointment in your practice. For your physiotherapy practice, this creates an expanded offering that makes your care more holistic and clearly sets you apart from other practices. Patients who feel that you think beyond the treatment itself are happy to come back and recommend you.

Genetics as a scientific foundation

Everyone utilizes nutrients differently — it's written in the genes. A DNA analysis from Novogenia shows how your patients absorb and process individual micronutrients. That lets you give personalized recommendations based on individual predisposition rather than on generic averages. This genetics in physiotherapy is a strong differentiator and builds genuine trust with your patients. You position yourself as a modern, scientifically minded practice and stand out from providers who simply sell standard products.

White-label: products under your own brand

Your patients trust you — not an unknown manufacturer. That's why all products are available as white-label: DNA tests and micronutrients appear under your logo, your label and your packaging. Novogenia stays in the background as the producer and never appears to the outside. So with every product you build your own practice brand, instead of promoting someone else's. That's the core of a genuine white-label solution for physiotherapists. Your brand accompanies your patients home and reminds them of your practice with every use.

Three ways to start with Novogenia

You choose how to get started. First: finished products to resell — proven analyses and micronutrients, ready to use with no lead time. Second: white-label under your brand — the same high-quality products with your branding. Third: your own product idea — you bring the concept or formulation, we develop and produce it. All three paths start with a no-obligation conversation and can be tailored to your practice's needs. You can start small and expand your offering step by step, without committing from the very beginning.

Starting from small batch — begin without high risk

High minimum quantities are often an obstacle for a practice and tie up capital unnecessarily. Thanks to modern technology, Novogenia produces from small batch. You start with a small batch, gain experience with your patients, and only scale up once demand is there. That lowers the risk and makes entering your own micronutrient line predictable. Especially for a growing physiotherapy practice, this flexibility is valuable, because you don't have to finance dead stock and can adjust your range at any time.

Made in Austria — quality that builds trust

Production in Austria stands for strict EU requirements, tested raw materials and full traceability. Made in Austria is therefore a strong argument with your patients. Novogenia has been developing and producing in its own certified genetic lab in Salzburg since 2009. This connection of science and production makes us a reliable partner for your physiotherapy practice. Short distances between analysis and manufacturing mean more control, consistent quality and fair prices, because no middlemen take a cut.

More value for your patients — and your practice

Personalized micronutrients and genetically grounded recommendations make your physiotherapy practice not only more holistic but also more attractive commercially. You expand your offering beyond treatment on the table and give your patients something they use regularly. That creates an additional, recurring revenue stream through repeat orders while strengthening patient loyalty. A practice that works with modern genetics and individual care clearly stands out from competitors who offer only classic therapy.
