Nutritionist

Personalized weight coaching

A nutritionist registers and orders the Weight-Regulation analysis for her clients, then uses it to tailor each client’s nutrition strategy to their individual genetic weight-regulation profile.

Physiotherapist

Genetics for athletes

A physiotherapist who treats athletes adds performance & training-optimization analyses as a premium service. He runs his own shop and sends clients there to order it themselves.

Insurance

Free analyses for members

An insurer offers analyses free to its members. Customers sign up with Novogenia, we ship the kit and run the analysis, and send the results back. The member gets their results; the insurer only gets the invoice — members live healthier and the insurer saves long-term.

Company / brand

Genetics under your brand

A company already selling healthcare or nutrition products integrates Novogenia’s genetic analyses under its own brand, strengthening its existing product portfolio.

Cosmetic studio

Gene-tailored beauty program

A cosmetic studio stands out from competitors with a gene-adapted analysis and cosmetics program. They take the samples on-site, send them in, and hand the finished personalized products to the customer.

Pharmacy

Analyses & supplements at the counter

A pharmacy differentiates itself with personalized analyses and supplements. The sample is taken in-store and sent to the lab; the customer returns to collect their products — and comes back every three months as new supplements are prepared.
