Knowledge & Guide

Laboratory services for hospitals — the complete overview.

Why an external outsourcing lab?

Hospitals are under growing cost and efficiency pressure. Building and operating your own genetic laboratory for DNA analyses is expensive, regulatorily demanding and ties up specialized staff who are scarce on the job market. Equipment investments, validation, maintenance and ongoing quality assurance create fixed costs that only amortize at very high throughput.

An experienced outsourcing lab offers a resource-efficient alternative here: the genetic analysis is outsourced while the hospital concentrates on its core tasks. High fixed costs become predictable costs per analysis, and responsibility for lab operation lies with the specialized partner. Novogenia has been active as a genetic lab since 2009 and operates its own certified EU lab in Salzburg.

What is a contract laboratory service?

With a laboratory service on a contract basis, an external specialist takes over the entire analysis — from sample intake to the structured report. The hospital needs neither sequencing equipment nor cleanroom technology nor its own lab team. Instead, it draws on the infrastructure, expertise and established, quality-assured processes of an experienced producer.

The sample-to-report logistics follow a clearly defined, traceable workflow. For the hospital this means organizational relief: it need not maintain internal lab structures, service no equipment and tie up no specialist staff, but instead receives a clear, structured report at the end — on time and at consistent quality.

Sample logistics: the end-to-end process

A key advantage of an experienced partner laboratory is its end-to-end sample logistics. Novogenia supplies sample kits, organizes shipping and handles incoming inspection, analysis and report delivery. This closed process ensures traceable workflows and takes organizational load off your hospital — without having to build additional internal structures.

Standardized sample kits and defined shipping routes reduce sources of error and ensure that every sample reaches the lab correctly identified and documented. Especially in a hospital with many wards involved, such a clearly regulated, end-to-end workflow is decisive for reliability and quality.

White-label for hospitals

On request, we provide the laboratory service in white-label: analyses and reports appear under your hospital's name and layout, while Novogenia stays in the background as an outsourcing lab. This creates a consistent appearance toward patients, while the technical and scientific processing rests with the reliable partner laboratory.

The genetic offering is thus perceived as part of your own range of services — an advantage for external image and patient loyalty. The structure and tone of the reports can be adapted to your hospital's standards so they fit into your existing patient communication.

Scalable capacity for any volume

A hospital's sample volume can fluctuate sharply. Our scalable capacity is designed for this: whether individual orders, a pilot project or ongoing operation with high case volumes — we flexibly adapt processes and quantities. The partner having its own lab means predictable workflows, short paths and full control over quality.

This scalability enables a low-risk entry: you can first trial the collaboration on a limited scale and only increase volume once demand is confirmed. Even as case numbers grow, quality remains constant thanks to standardized processes — a reliability that is indispensable in clinical operation.

Quality, safety and “Made in Austria”

Genetic analyses are subject to strict requirements in the EU. Novogenia produces in its own certified facility in Salzburg to EU standard and ensures traceable, documented workflows as well as complete sample traceability.

An analysis in Austria or the EU — Made in Austria — is not only a quality argument but also a trust argument toward your patients. Short paths to the lab, a clear point of contact and transparent processes also make internal coordination and answering follow-up questions easier.

Personalized supplements as a complement

Beyond pure DNA analysis, Novogenia can on request produce personalized supplements as an own brand. Based on the analysis results, we manufacture individually tailored supplements — entirely under your hospital's branding and from our own EU lab in Salzburg.

Thanks to advanced galenics such as slow-release, pellet technology and gastric-acid-resistant capsules, even demanding active-ingredient combinations can be implemented precisely. Analysis and product thus combine into an end-to-end offering from a single source — without you having to coordinate additional suppliers, and with the same reliable quality as the lab services.
