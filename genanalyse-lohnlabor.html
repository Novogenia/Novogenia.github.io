Knowledge & Guide

Outsourcing genetic analyses — the complete overview.

What does a contract lab for genetic analyses mean?

A contract lab — also called an outsourcing lab or partner laboratory — performs genetic analyses on behalf of another company. Instead of building your own genetic lab, you outsource the genetic analysis to a specialized service provider that has the instruments, methods and qualified staff. You supply samples or the research question, and the contract lab returns reproducible results.

The appeal is obvious: operating your own genetic lab ties up substantial investment in instruments, cleanroom technology and skilled staff. If you only need genetic analyses on a project basis or as part of a larger product, an outsourcing lab is far more economical — and keeps you flexible. For exactly this, Novogenia operates its own, certified EU genetic lab in Salzburg and has been active as a genetic lab since 2009.

For whom is outsourcing DNA testing worthwhile?

Outsourcing genetic analyses is attractive for many players. These include brands and providers of health or DNA tests that don't want to run their own lab, as well as diagnostics and biotech companies with short-term capacity needs. Research institutions and companies that only require genotyping on a project basis also benefit from an external laboratory service.

They all have one thing in common: they focus on their product, their brand or their study — while the contract lab handles the analytical work. Especially at market entry, outsourcing DNA testing significantly reduces risk, because you don't have to maintain equipment or staff while demand is still uncertain. As the business grows, the volume at your partner laboratory can simply be scaled up.

Which analyses does a genetic-analysis contract lab handle?

The spectrum ranges from pure DNA extraction through targeted SNP genotyping to high-throughput processing of large sample volumes. Novogenia has developed more than 30 of its own genetic tests and brings that methodological expertise to every project.

You can outsource individual steps or hand over the entire process from sample to finished report — depending on what you want to cover yourself. Some clients already supply extracted DNA and only need the genotyping; others hand over raw saliva samples and leave extraction, analysis and evaluation to us. This modularity is what makes a contract lab so versatile: it fits exactly into the gap that is open in your own process.

Sample logistics: from saliva to the lab

A frequently underestimated part of the genetic analysis is the logistics. Samples have to be safely collected, clearly assigned, shipped and registered before the analysis can even begin. Mistakes at this stage — a mixed-up sample, an interrupted transport — can no longer be corrected in the lab.

As your partner laboratory, on request we organize the entire sample logistics — from branded saliva collection kits through shipping to sample intake and registration in the system. That gives you a continuous chain without handover losses, instead of having to coordinate several service providers. Every sample remains clearly traceable, which safeguards data integrity across the entire project.

High throughput and scalability

Anyone who outsources DNA testing usually doesn't just want to process a few samples but to scale reliably. Automated workflows enable high-throughput processing of large sample volumes at consistent quality — an advantage a small in-house lab rarely achieves.

At Novogenia you start with a test run and scale up the ongoing contract analysis as your volume grows — without reproducibility or turnaround suffering. This elasticity is decisive precisely for seasonal businesses or fast-growing brands: the lab breathes with demand instead of limiting it. Standardized processes ensure that the thousandth sample is treated exactly like the first.

Quality, data formats and “Made in Austria”

Genetic analyses demand seamless traceability and reproducible results. Look for a contract lab that operates in a certified facility to EU standard and guarantees end-to-end documentation. At Novogenia, analysis and evaluation take place in Austria — Made in Austria is not only a quality argument but also a trust argument toward your end customers.

Just as important as the analysis itself is the form in which you receive the results. We deliver them in your preferred format, whether as structured raw data for further processing in your own pipeline or as a finished report. That way the results can be incorporated into your platform, your report or your evaluation without friction.

What to look for when choosing a partner laboratory

Not every outsourcing lab is the same. Anyone who outsources genetic analyses hands over a sensitive part of the value chain and should choose the partner carefully. A first criterion is whether the lab actually performs the analysis itself or merely passes the samples on — an own, certified genetic lab stands for clear responsibility instead of anonymous subcontracting.

Equally important are methodological experience, the reproducibility of the results, scalability at high throughput, and the question of whether the sample logistics also come from a single source. A partner laboratory that covers the entire chain from sample to report spares you the coordination of several service providers and reduces the interfaces where errors arise. Novogenia meets these criteria with its own EU genetic lab and over 15 years of experience.
