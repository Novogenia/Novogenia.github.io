Knowledge & Guide

DNA tests & own brands in the pharmacy — the complete overview.

Why DNA tests and supplements belong in your pharmacy range

For many people, the pharmacy is the first port of call for health questions — and therefore the ideal place for modern, scientifically grounded products. DNA tests in the pharmacy and matching personalized supplements combine exactly what pharmacies stand for: personal advice, tested quality and individual recommendations. By expanding your range with such premium products, you create genuine added value in consultations and rise above pure price competition.

There is also a commercial angle: your own brand of DNA tests, supplements and cosmetics ties customers to your pharmacy rather than to interchangeable manufacturer products. The genetic test becomes the entry point to personal health advice that naturally leads to follow-up products — a range with real repeat-purchase potential instead of a one-off sale.

What does white label mean for pharmacies?

With a white-label solution you supply the idea or the desired range, and Novogenia produces the products under your logo, label and packaging. This creates your own pharmacy own brand, without you needing your own lab, machinery or approvals. Your customers see only your brand — we never appear on the outside.

This turns a formulation into a ready-to-market, exclusive branded product. You focus on advice, brand and sales in your dispensary, while we take care of the entire development, production and contract filling. This clear division of labour is the reason why private label is the fastest route to your own line for many pharmacies.

DNA tests as a consultation-driven entry product

A DNA test is more than a product — it is a conversation starter. Genetic analyses on topics such as nutrition, metabolism or skin ageing open up a personal advisory conversation and lead naturally to the right personalized supplements. This creates a well-thought-out range pathway: from the test through the individual recommendation to the follow-up purchase — all under your own brand.

In the pharmacy in particular, where trust and expertise are central, a scientifically grounded genetic test fits perfectly. It positions your pharmacy as a modern health partner and sets you clearly apart from competitors who only stock standard products on the shelf.

Personalized supplements & cosmetics from the EU lab

Novogenia produces personalized supplements in many dosage forms — from capsules and tablets through powders and sachets to slow-release, pellet technology and gastro-resistant capsules. Add to that personalized cosmetics based on the genetic profile. Everything is made in our own certified EU lab in Salzburg — Made in Austria, tested to EU standards and with complete traceability.

This sophisticated galenics is a genuine mark of quality: gastro-resistant capsules carry acid-sensitive actives safely through to the small intestine, while slow-release formulations ensure a steady supply over hours. Such dosage forms are far from standard among suppliers — and they are exactly what signals true pharmacy quality to your customers. The result is a range that lives up to the pharmaceutical standard of your pharmacy down to the detail and stands out clearly from ordinary drugstore stock.

From small batches up, not vast minimum quantities

Many traditional suppliers demand high minimum order quantities. Thanks to modern processes, Novogenia produces from small batches up — ideal for first testing a new pharmacy range with a manageable batch. If demand is there, we scale together up to large-scale production. This reduces your risk and protects your cash flow, especially when entering your own-brand business.

This means you don't have to invest in large stock holdings before you know how your own brand will be received. You can trial a first test batch in your pharmacy, gather your customers' feedback and build the range step by step — a predictable, low-risk path to your own line.

Three ways to your pharmacy range

The choice is yours: use Novogenia as a white-label manufacturer for your own brand, bring your own product idea and we'll realise it, or source proven Novogenia products as finished goods to resell. This way the model fits every pharmacy — from the single dispensary to the pharmacy group.

Which route is right depends on your goals: if you want to build a strong own brand, go for white label; if you want to start quickly, begin with finished products and switch to your own brand later. We are happy to advise you on which model best fits your pharmacy and your target group.
