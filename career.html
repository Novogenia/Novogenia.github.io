Good to know

Application FAQ.

📎Which file formats can I upload?▾
DOC, PDF, JPG, PNG and GIF up to 7 MB. For larger files (e.g. graphic samples) you can share a Dropbox link or similar.
✨Can I send a speculative application?▾
Absolutely. Click the speculative-application button and upload your CV — you might be the perfect fit for our team.
📄Applying for several positions?▾
Please send your CV only once. If you’d like to be considered for multiple openings, just mention it in your cover letter.
🔒What happens to my data?▾
A profile is created in our applicant database with technical and organizational security measures. After the process your documents are kept for at least seven months and then deleted.
💬How does the interview work?▾
Depending on the position: a phone interview, an on-site individual or group interview, or a video interview (usually via Microsoft Teams). You’ll receive detailed instructions with your invitation.
