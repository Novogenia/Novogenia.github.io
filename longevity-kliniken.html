Knowledge & Guide

Partner lab for longevity clinics — the complete overview.

Why genetic analyses in longevity medicine?

Modern longevity clinics rely on individualized strategies rather than blanket recommendations. Genetic analyses provide an important foundation for this: they make visible how a person processes certain nutrients, how they respond to lifestyle factors and where individual needs exist.

A specialized partner laboratory takes over these genetic analyses, while the clinic concentrates on counseling and guidance. This lets you build a scientifically grounded longevity offering without investing in your own genetic laboratory. Novogenia has been active as a genetic lab since 2009 and operates its own certified EU lab in Salzburg — with the experience to reliably implement demanding analysis concepts. From the combination of scientific expertise and in-house production emerges a partner that not only delivers data but can also translate it into concrete products.

Genetic analyses and biomarkers as a foundation

Individual longevity programs rest on a sound data basis. Genetic analyses around relevant biomarkers reveal genetically determined strengths and needs — for example in metabolism, in the processing of certain nutrients or in the body's own protective mechanisms.

These insights can be translated into concrete, personalized recommendations and combined with further data from your clinic concept. Important here: genetic analyses provide scientific reference points for individualized concepts, not promises of cure. The professional interpretation and counseling remain the responsibility of your longevity clinic — we provide the robust analytical foundation for it.

From biological age to an individual strategy

The concept of biological age is moving to the center of longevity medicine: it describes how “old” a body functions compared to its chronological age. Genetic predisposition is one of several factors here, alongside lifestyle, nutrition and environment.

A sound genetic analysis helps to identify individual needs and align your clinic's longevity strategy accordingly — as a building block of a holistic, data-supported approach. This allows recommendations to be formulated more precisely and the counseling outcome to be followed in a traceable way over time, rather than working with blanket programs for everyone. This positions your clinic as a scientifically grounded provider that takes individual data seriously and translates it into concrete, personalized measures.

Personalized longevity supplements

Insights from the analysis only unfold their value once concrete products emerge from them. Novogenia produces personalized longevity supplements — individually composed supplements based on the analysis results, entirely under your clinic's own brand.

Thanks to advanced galenics such as slow-release, pellet technology and gastric-acid-resistant capsules, even demanding active-ingredient combinations can be implemented precisely. Analysis and product thus combine into an end-to-end offering from a single source: the report leads not only to a recommendation, but to a high-quality, tangible product your clients can use directly.

White-label for longevity clinics

With a white-label solution, genetic analyses, reports and supplements appear under your longevity clinic's name. Logo, layout and communication carry your brand, while Novogenia stays in the background as a scientific partner laboratory.

This creates a consistent, high-quality brand experience for your clients — from analysis to finished product. Especially in the premium segment of longevity medicine, where brand and trust play a central role, this end-to-end brand management is a clear advantage over the mere resale of outside standard products. The structure, tone and appearance of the reports can be closely adapted to your clinic's standards.

Joint product development & scaling

Beyond standard solutions, we develop tailor-made concepts with you: specific analysis packages or matching product lines for your longevity approach. In a structured process, we translate your professional requirements into concrete, actionable offerings.

The scalable capacity of our EU lab grows with your clinic — from an individual program to high throughput. The partner having its own lab means predictable workflows, short paths and full control over quality, so you can trial the offering with low risk and expand it flexibly as demand rises.

Advanced galenics for longevity active ingredients

Many active ingredients relevant to longevity medicine place particular demands on processing. This is where Novogenia's advanced galenics pay off: slow-release formulations release active ingredients over hours in a delayed manner, instead of short peaks; pellet technology allows the individual combination of several micronutrients; and gastric-acid-resistant capsules carry acid-labile active ingredients protected into the small intestine.

Such demanding dosage forms are by no means offered by every manufacturer. For your longevity clinic, this means the ability to implement products that clearly stand out technologically from the mass market — and thus a quality promise that fits a high-quality, individualized longevity offering.
