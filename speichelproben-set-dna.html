Knowledge & Guide

Saliva collection kits for DNA — the overview.

What is a saliva collection kit for DNA testing?

A saliva collection kit — also called a DNA sample collection kit or sample collection kit — makes it possible to collect a saliva sample simply and cleanly, usually conveniently at home. From the saliva, the lab obtains the DNA that serves as the basis for every DNA analysis.

A good saliva collection set is designed so that collection succeeds reliably even without trained staff and the sample is transported stably to the lab. This is often where it's decided whether an analysis can be carried out at all: a wrongly collected or spoiled-in-transit sample is no longer usable. Novogenia delivers such kits from its own EU genetic lab — tuned to a smooth lab process.

Medical device or non-medical device?

Saliva collection kits can be classified as a medical device or as a non-medical device — the right variant depends on the intended purpose of your application. This classification is not just a formality; it determines the framework in which you may offer and advertise your product.

For applications that require a medical classification, we deliver the medical-device variant. For lifestyle, wellness or research applications without a medical intended purpose, the non-medical-device variant is suitable. Which classification is right for your use case we clarify together at the start — so that your kit fits its intended use from the very beginning.

Branded kits as a brand experience

The collection kit is often the first physical touchpoint between your customer and your product. Long before any findings are available, the customer holds your kit in their hands — and forms a judgment about your brand.

A branded kit with your logo, clear instructions and high-quality packaging turns a functional kit into a brand experience. That way the customer journey of your DNA test begins as early as unboxing — not only with the findings. An understandable set of instructions also reduces the number of incorrectly collected samples, which benefits both your customers and the lab process. On request, we fully assemble the kits in your design.

Assembly and ship-readiness

A ready-to-use kit consists of more than the sample tube: instructions, packaging, return-shipping material and clear labeling all belong to it. Each of these elements has to fit together so that the kit works at the customer's end and the sample comes back safely.

In assembly, we put together fully stocked, ship-ready kits, so that you don't have to coordinate several suppliers and packaging steps. You receive kits you can ship directly to your end customers or study participants — without anyone having to lend a hand in your own house. That saves logistics effort and ensures that every delivered kit is complete and correctly stocked.

From sample to findings from a single source

The greatest advantage arises when the saliva collection kit and the analysis come from the same house. Then sample collection, sample logistics and genetic analysis mesh seamlessly — from collection at home through sample intake to the finished findings.

Novogenia offers this seamless chain: on request, the kits are linked directly to our logistics and our lab, which avoids handover losses and simplifies the process for you and your customers. Instead of buying a kit from one provider and the analysis from another and coordinating the two, you get a solution that's matched to itself. Every sample stays clearly assignable from the first step to the last.

Quality and “Made in Austria”

The quality of a collection kit is part of what determines whether the later analysis succeeds. A cheap kit that doesn't keep the DNA stable jeopardizes every subsequent genetic analysis — regardless of how well the lab works.

So look for kits that are assembled in a facility with its own lab and to EU standard. At Novogenia, assembly and analysis take place in our certified lab in Salzburg — Made in Austria. That way the entire chain from sample to findings stays under clear quality responsibility, instead of being split across several suppliers. This origin is also a trust argument that you can pass on to your end customers.

For which applications are the kits suitable?

Saliva collection kits are the entry ticket to nearly every DNA analysis that works without a blood draw. Their range of use is correspondingly broad: providers of DNA tests and personalized health products use them, as do resellers building their own test line, or research teams collecting samples from large study populations.

Depending on the application, the variant as a medical device or as a non-medical device is used. For lifestyle, wellness and research applications without a medical intended purpose, the non-medical-device variant is generally sufficient; applications with a medical character require the corresponding classification. Because we deliver both variants from our own EU genetic lab and, on request, link them directly to the analysis, the right sample collection kit can be assembled for almost any project — branded, ship-ready and cleanly integrated into the lab process.
