Knowledge & Guide

DNA analysis & personalized supplements in nutrition consulting — the complete overview.

Why nutrigenetics is changing nutrition consulting

Classic nutrition consulting works with general reference values and average recommendations. But everyone metabolizes nutrients differently — and that is genetically determined. This is exactly where nutrigenetics comes in: a DNA analysis shows how your clients respond to fats, carbohydrates, vitamins and minerals. That lets you build nutrition plans that truly fit each person's predisposition — instead of guessing or relying on generic tables. For your practice this means a clear unique selling point and scientifically grounded advice that wins your clients over and visibly improves their results. Once someone understands that their own genetics help determine which diet works, they are happy to come back.

Personalized supplements for your practice

After the analysis comes the implementation. With personalized supplements you give your clients exactly the micronutrients that sensibly complement their diet — no more and no less. Novogenia produces these tailor-made blends in our own EU lab, matched to the DNA result and the individual nutrition goal. This turns pure consulting into a complete offering: analysis, recommendation and product from a single source. It noticeably strengthens client loyalty and creates an additional, recurring revenue stream for your nutrition practice. Instead of sending your clients to a third-party online shop, the value stays within your practice.

White-label: your own brand instead of someone else's logos

Your clients should trust you — not an unfamiliar manufacturer. That's why we offer all products as white-label: DNA tests and supplements appear under your logo, your label and your packaging. Novogenia stays in the background as the producer and never appears to the outside. So with every product sold you build your own brand, instead of promoting someone else's. That's the decisive difference between simple reselling and a genuine white-label solution for nutritionists. Your brand becomes the hallmark of your expertise and accompanies your clients all the way home.

Three ways to start with Novogenia

You decide how deep you want to go. First: finished products to resell — proven DNA analyses and supplements, ready to use with no lead time. Second: white-label under your brand — the same high-quality products, but with your branding and packaging. Third: your own product idea — you bring the concept or desired formulation, we develop and produce it for you. All three paths start with a no-obligation conversation and can be combined flexibly, depending on how your practice develops. So you don't have to commit from the start — you can grow step by step.

Starting from small batch — begin without high risk

Many manufacturers demand high minimum quantities and force you to take large batches. Thanks to modern technology, Novogenia produces from small batch — ideal for nutritionists who want to test first. You start with a small batch, gain experience with your clients, and only scale up once demand is there. That protects your cash flow and significantly lowers the risk of building your own product line. Especially when entering the market, this flexibility is decisive, because you don't have to finance dead stock and can adjust your range at any time.

Made in Austria — quality your clients can feel

Production in Austria is more than a seal. Made in Austria stands for strict EU requirements, tested raw materials and complete traceability — a strong argument for trust with your clients. Novogenia has been developing and producing in its own certified genetic lab in Salzburg since 2009. From this combination of science and production comes a partner that doesn't just fill bottles but truly masters nutrigenetics. Short distances between analysis and production also mean more control, consistent quality and fair prices, because no middlemen take a cut.

More value for your clients — and for your practice

The combination of DNA analysis and personalized supplements makes your nutrition practice not only more scientific but also more attractive commercially. You no longer just sell your time — you offer a complete program of testing, consulting and matching products. That increases the value of every consultation, improves your clients' results and creates a recurring revenue stream through repeat orders. At the same time you strengthen your position as a knowledgeable point of contact for individual nutrition — an advantage that generic advice without a genetic basis simply can't offer.
