Knowledge & Guide

Genetic analyses & personalized cosmetics in the spa — the complete overview.

Why personalization will define the spa of tomorrow

The wellness market is becoming noticeably more demanding: today clients expect individual, scientifically grounded care instead of interchangeable treatments. Standardized treatments and off-the-shelf care products are no longer enough to differentiate in the upscale segment. Genetic analyses in the spa and personalized cosmetics meet exactly this expectation. They connect the growing desire for beauty and self-care with real science — turning a treatment into a tailored experience. Anyone offering a skin analysis and matching care sets their spa & wellness facility clearly apart from the standard and wins discerning regular clients. An interchangeable wellness visit becomes an individual advisory experience your guests remember and recommend.

What a genetic skin analysis delivers

From a simple saliva sample, our own lab determines genetic predispositions that are decisive for the skin: the individual tendency toward skin aging, the moisture balance, collagen formation and personal protection against oxidative stress. These insights form the basis for targeted treatments and a matching at-home care routine. A general consultation thus becomes a sound, personal recommendation — added value that goes well beyond classic skin diagnostics with measuring devices, because it accounts for genetic predisposition, which does not change over the course of life. You can align care and treatments not only with the current skin condition but with long-term predisposition — more precise and more sustainable than any snapshot.

Personalized cosmetics as your own brand

Based on the analysis, we deliver personalized cosmetics matched to your clients' skin type and genetics — fully as white-label products under your spa brand. All products carry your logo, label and packaging design. The result is an exclusive care line that your clients associate solely with your house. You supply the idea and the brand, Novogenia supplies the science and the production from our own lab. This makes your care line a genuine unique selling point that competitors cannot simply copy — and a high-quality retail product for your spa boutique.

Combining care from the outside and the inside

Beautiful skin doesn't come from cosmetics alone. With personalized beauty supplements for skin, hair and vitality you complement your treatments from within. This combination of personalized cosmetics and matching supplements is a strong premium argument — and at the same time creates a second pillar for your revenue, because clients keep receiving the products long-term after their appointment. A single treatment thus becomes a continuous care relationship that binds your clients to your facility long-term and ensures predictable follow-up revenue.

Start from small batch — without risk

You don't have to launch a large care line all at once. Thanks to modern production methods in our own lab, manufacturing is possible from small batch. So you first test your line with a manageable range, gather your clients' feedback, and only scale once demand is there. This makes entering personalized cosmetics predictable and protects your liquidity. High minimum quantities or expensive inventory, as many classic cosmetics manufacturers require, are eliminated — you take no unnecessary risk and can let your offering grow organically.

Quality & trust: Made in Austria

In the beauty and health field, credibility counts. Novogenia operates its own certified lab in Salzburg and produces Made in Austria to EU standard. That is a strong trust argument toward your clients, who increasingly pay attention to origin, purity and verified quality in care products. An own lab also means shorter routes, complete traceability, full control over quality and fair prices, because no middlemen take a cut. So you get premium quality at an honest price — and a convincing argument for your consultation.

How you integrate personalization into your treatments

The rollout succeeds without major effort. Sample collection takes only a few minutes and integrates easily into your existing consultation or treatment conversation. Novogenia handles the genetic evaluation and the entire production — your team needs no scientific expertise. We support you with training, materials and integration into your treatments and care concepts. Whether as an exclusive anti-aging program, a personalized take-home care routine, or an advisory add-on: the skin analysis becomes the natural starting point of every high-quality treatment and noticeably raises the perceived value of your treatments.
