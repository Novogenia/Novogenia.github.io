Knowledge & Guide

Gene tests & personalized stacks in biohacking — the complete overview.

Why genetics is the missing data layer in biohacking

Biohacking and quantified self thrive on measured data: sleep, heart-rate variability, glucose, blood values. Wearables and trackers today deliver a flood of real-time data. Yet one decisive, static data set is missing from most setups — genetics. It explains why two people respond completely differently to the same supplementation, the same training or the same diet. A gene test delivers exactly this missing foundation and turns generic optimization into real, individual personalization. For any biohacking or longevity community this is a strong differentiator against superficial standard tests that only scratch the surface. Genetic data give every optimization decision a stable foundation — and set your offering apart from purely trend-driven providers.

What gene tests and biomarkers reveal

From a simple saliva sample, our own lab analyzes genetic biomarkers relevant to performance and health: nutrient utilization, metabolism, caffeine breakdown, detoxification capacity, oxidative protection and predispositions around aging. These genetic insights end the tedious trial-and-error: instead of blindly trying supplements and waiting for effects, your community knows on a data basis what their own body responds to — the essence of serious biohacking. Unlike dynamic measurements, genetic data remain valid for life: once analyzed, they serve as a permanent reference for every future optimization decision. This makes the gene test a one-time investment with lifelong benefit — a convincing argument for any ambitious community.

Personalized supplement stacks instead of standard blends

Based on the genetic data we assemble personalized supplement stacks — matched to the individual profile instead of a one-size-fits-all approach. This is the decisive difference from generic multivitamin products: each stack addresses exactly the areas where the genetic profile actually shows a need, and leaves out what the body already utilizes sufficiently. As white-label products under your brand, these stacks become the exclusive centerpiece of your offering — a product that stands clearly apart from interchangeable supplements on the market and lets your community experience real, traceable personalization.

Longevity supplements with advanced galenics

In the longevity field, it's not only which active ingredients are included that matters, but also how they get into the body and whether they actually arrive at the site of action. Novogenia masters demanding galenics: slow-release for constant release over hours instead of short peaks, pellet technology for individually combinable micronutrients, and gastro-resistant capsules for acid-sensitive ingredients such as probiotics or sulforaphane. The result is longevity supplements that go well beyond simple capsules — a quality marker that demanding biohackers appreciate and that many providers on the market simply cannot deliver.

You decide how deep you go. With the white-label solution, you supply the idea and the brand, Novogenia supplies science and production — all tests and stacks carry your logo, label and design. Alternatively, you take finished products straight into your shop or program without developing anything yourself. Both are possible from small batch, so you can test your offering at low risk and scale organically with your community. This flexibility makes getting started easy — whether you run an online community, a coaching program, a studio or a shop.

Quality & trust: Made in Austria

A data-driven, critical audience especially pays meticulous attention to seriousness and origin. Novogenia operates its own certified genetic lab in Salzburg and produces Made in Austria to EU standard. That is a strong trust argument toward biohackers who question every source. An own lab also means full control over data quality and production, complete traceability of raw materials, and fair prices, because no middlemen take a cut. So your community gets verified premium quality — and you gain an argument that holds up to even the most demanding discussion within your community.

How you integrate gene tests into your offering

Integration is straightforward and flexible. Your members receive a test kit, provide a saliva sample and send it in — the evaluation takes place in our lab. You can seamlessly integrate the results report and the matching stacks into your existing program, shop or app. We accompany you from concept through branding to logistics. Whether as a premium tier in your membership, an onboarding building block for new members, or a standalone product: the gene test becomes the natural starting point of every data-driven optimization journey and firmly anchors your brand in your community's everyday life.
