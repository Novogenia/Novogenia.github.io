Knowledge & Guide

Personalized health products in retail — the complete overview.

Why personalized health products belong on the retail shelf

Health is one of the strongest growth trends in retail and grocery retail. Stocking a distinctive line of personalized health products here — DNA tests, supplements and cosmetics — sets you apart from interchangeable standard stock and creates genuine added value on the shelf. Novogenia supplies exactly this line as white label, ready for listing under your own brand.

Personalized products address the growing need for individual health and attract more attention on the shelf than anonymous mass-market goods. For retail, that means a more attractive margin and an own brand that deliberately ties customers to your sales channel. This turns an interchangeable health shelf into a distinctive line with recognition value that strengthens your brand instead of advertising third-party manufacturers — a clear strategic advantage in a market where ranges otherwise barely differ.

What does a white-label line in grocery retail mean?

With a white-label line you supply the idea or the desired range, and Novogenia produces the products under your logo, label and packaging. This creates a grocery own brand, without your own lab or production facilities. Your customers see only your brand — we stay in the background as the manufacturer.

This lets your team focus on buying, listing and marketing, while we take care of development, production and filling. This clear division of labour makes it possible to build a fully fledged health line without having to invest in production infrastructure yourself — a decisive advantage when fast time-to-shelf and predictable costs are required.

DNA tests as a highlight on the health shelf

A DNA test is an exceptional shelf product: it draws attention, conveys innovation and opens the way to follow-up products such as matching personalized supplements. This creates a well-thought-out range pathway from the test through the recommendation to the repeat purchase — all under your line.

On the health shelf, where many products look similar, a genetic test is a clear eye-catcher and a strong argument for your own brand. It lifts the entire line to a higher level of quality and innovation and makes it especially interesting for health-conscious customers. The DNA test thus becomes the flagship that draws attention to the whole range and carries the sell-through of the follow-up products.

Supplements and cosmetics from the EU lab

Novogenia produces supplements in many dosage forms — capsules, tablets, powders, sachets and, on request, slow-release or pellet technology — as well as personalized cosmetics. Everything is made in our own certified EU lab in Salzburg: Made in Austria, tested to EU standards and with complete traceability — important arguments for buyers and end customers alike.

In retail in particular, reliable, consistent quality across large volumes is what counts. Our own lab ensures stable formulations, consistent batch quality and transparent origin — qualities that convince both in the buying conversation and on the packaging. The result is a range that holds the same high standard at rising unit numbers as it did in the very first batch.

Scalable from the pilot shelf to large-scale production

Retail needs scalability. Novogenia produces from the pilot shelf in a few locations to nationwide listing — reliably and in consistent quality. This way you can first test a new line regionally and, if successful, scale up to large-scale production without having to change suppliers.

This staged roll-out significantly reduces your listing risk: you gather sell-through data within a manageable scope and only roll out broadly once the line has proven itself. We grow with your volume and remain available at all times — from the first test market to supplying a nationwide store network.

Three ways to your retail line

You choose your model: Novogenia as a white-label manufacturer for your own brand, your own product idea that we realise, or proven Novogenia products as finished goods to resell. This way the concept fits every retail format — from specialist retail to the grocery chain.

Whether you want to build a complete own-brand line or first list individual products — we align the model with your format, your volume and your marketing strategy and advise you on the right range depth. This creates a line that fits exactly your target group and your shelf space, instead of an off-the-shelf range.
