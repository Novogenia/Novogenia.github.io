Knowledge & Guide

Personalized care products & private label for foot care & podiatry — the complete overview.

Why your own care line strengthens your practice

An own product line is a real growth lever for foot care and podiatry. It creates recognition, builds client loyalty and positions you as a specialist. With personalized care products under your own private label, you give your clients care to take home that they can only get from you. This extends the effect of your treatment beyond the appointment and creates an additional, recurring revenue stream. Every product with your name reminds your clients of your practice with daily use and strengthens the bond.

Contract manufacturing: your idea, our production

You don't need to own a lab to offer your own care products. Through contract manufacturing, Novogenia handles the entire production: you bring the idea or the desired formulation, we develop, produce and fill. This creates high-quality care products without high upfront investment — from the first foot cream to a complete care line for your podiatry practice. You draw on the infrastructure and know-how of an experienced manufacturer and focus entirely on your clients and your treatment.

White-label private label instead of someone else's logos

Your clients should associate your practice with good care — not an unknown manufacturer. That's why we produce all products as white-label: creams, oils and care products appear under your logo, your label and your packaging. Novogenia stays in the background as the producer. This creates a true private label with which you elevate your foot care service over the long term, instead of promoting someone else's brands. Your clients aren't buying just any product from the drugstore — they're buying your product, and that noticeably sets your practice apart from the competition.

Three ways to your own care line

You decide how to get started. First: finished products to resell — proven care, ready to use with no lead time. Second: white-label under your brand — the same high-quality products with your branding. Third: your own product idea via contract manufacturing — you bring the concept or formulation, we produce it. All three paths start with a no-obligation conversation and can be tailored to your practice. You can start with a few products and gradually expand your care line into a complete range.

Starting from small batch — begin without inventory risk

High minimum quantities are often an obstacle for a practice and tie up capital unnecessarily. Thanks to modern technology, Novogenia produces from small batch — ideal for foot care and podiatry providers who want to test first. You start with a small batch, gain experience with your clients, and only scale up once demand is there. That keeps your inventory lean and significantly lowers the risk of building your private label. So you stay flexible and can adjust your range to your clients' needs at any time.

Made in Austria — quality that wins clients over

Production in Austria stands for strict EU requirements, tested raw materials and full traceability. Made in Austria is therefore a strong selling point with your clients. Novogenia has been developing and producing in its own certified lab in Salzburg since 2009. This connection of experience and in-house production makes us a reliable partner for your care products. Short distances and in-house manufacturing mean more control, consistent quality and fair prices, because no middlemen take a cut.

More value for your clients — and your practice

Your own line of personalized care products makes your foot care and podiatry not only distinctive but also more attractive commercially. You no longer just sell your time — you offer a complete care experience of treatment and matching products for home. This increases the value of every appointment, improves your clients' care results and creates a recurring revenue stream through repeat purchases. A true private label from your own contract manufacturing clearly sets your practice apart from competitors who only recommend anonymous drugstore goods.
