What’s inside

The actives — and what they target.

Each active is chosen and concentrated for the customer’s skin genetics. Tap any one to see the detail.

🧵Collagen activesGene: MMP1▼
The MMP1 gene controls how quickly collagen is broken down in the skin. Targeted peptides and supporting actives are dosed to help counter MMP-1-driven collagen loss where the genotype calls for it.
☀️UV protectionGene: MC1R▼
MC1R determines the skin’s natural defence against UV. Reduced-function variants get extra photoprotective and repair actives to compensate.
🛡️Antioxidant defenceSOD2 · GPX1 · NQO1▼
Vitamins C and E, selenium and Coenzyme Q10 neutralise the free radicals that age skin. Your antioxidant genes decide which routes work best — so the active mix is chosen accordingly.
🔋Coenzyme Q10Gene: NQO1▼
Q10 only becomes active once the NQO1 enzyme switches it on. For skin profiles that can’t activate it efficiently, alternative antioxidants are selected instead — no wasted active.
🌿Soothing & barrier activesInflammation▼
Calming and barrier-supporting actives address the inflammatory tendencies (TNF-α, IL6) that drive redness and accelerated photo-ageing in some skin types.

Science: Today there are already about 4 million scientific publications that have studied the effects of genes on the human body. Recommendations: The adaptations of micronutrient dosing, cosmetic formulation and dietary or lifestyle recommendations derived from these findings have not yet been confirmed by randomised, placebo-controlled studies for every genetic effect.
