Knowledge & Guide

Genetic analyses for clinics — the complete overview.

Why a partner laboratory for genetic analyses?

More and more clinics want to offer their patients genetic analyses — as part of prevention, nutritional counseling or individualized care. Building your own genetic laboratory, however, is expensive, regulatorily demanding and ties up highly specialized staff for years. Cleanroom technology, sequencing equipment, process validation and ongoing quality assurance create fixed costs that only pay off at very high case volumes. For most clinics, the economically sensible solution is a specialized partner laboratory.

An experienced outsourcing lab takes over the entire analysis, while your clinic concentrates on what it does best: counseling, indication assessment and personal care. Novogenia has been active as a genetic lab since 2009 and operates its own certified EU lab in Salzburg. This lets you build a high-quality genetic analysis offering without investing in your own lab infrastructure.

What does an outsourcing lab mean?

With an outsourcing lab (also called a contract or toll lab), you fully outsource the genetic analysis to an external specialist. You need neither cleanroom technology nor sequencing equipment nor your own team of molecular biologists. Instead, you draw on the infrastructure, expertise and established processes of an experienced producer — and thus on a proven quality assurance system that you would otherwise have to build and maintain yourself.

The sample-to-report logistics follow a clearly defined workflow — from sample collection at your clinic through shipping and incoming inspection to the finished, structured result. For your clinic this means predictable costs per analysis instead of high fixed costs, short paths for follow-up questions and a partner who takes responsibility for the entire lab process.

White-label: your brand front and center

With a white-label solution, genetic analyses and reports appear under your clinic's name. Logo, layout and patient communication carry your brand — the partner lab stays in the background. This creates a consistent brand experience for your patients, while the scientific and technical processing rests with Novogenia.

This clear separation builds trust and strengthens loyalty to your institution. Rather than communicating an outside brand, you present the genetic offering as an integral part of your range of services. Report layouts can be adapted in structure, tone and appearance to your clinic, so they fit seamlessly into your existing patient communication.

Joint product development

Beyond pure analysis, we develop tailor-made concepts with you. Whether a specific analysis package for a clinical focus area or a matching product line — joint product development combines Novogenia's scientific experience with your clinic's medical focus.

In a structured process, we jointly clarify the target group, scope of analysis and desired dosage form, and translate your professional requirements into a concrete, actionable offering. This produces solutions that clearly stand out from standard products and fit your care concept exactly — a genuine point of differentiation from institutions that merely pass on bought-in standard tests.

Personalized supplements as your own brand

Genetic analyses only deliver their value once concrete recommendations can be derived from them. Based on the results, Novogenia produces personalized supplements — individually composed supplements, entirely under your clinic's own brand. Analysis and product combine into an end-to-end offering from a single source, with no need to coordinate several suppliers.

Thanks to advanced galenics such as slow-release, pellet technology and gastric-acid-resistant capsules, even demanding active-ingredient combinations can be implemented precisely. For your clinic this means a meaningful extension of your range of services: the analysis leads not only to a report, but to a concrete, high-quality product that makes the counseling outcome visible and tangible.

Scalable capacity & reliable logistics

Whether a pilot project with a few samples or ongoing operation with high case volumes — our scalable capacity grows with your clinic's demand. The end-to-end sample logistics ensure traceable workflows from sample collection to report delivery. The partner having its own lab means shorter paths, more control and predictable processes.

This flexibility is especially valuable at the start: you can first trial the offering on a smaller scale and only increase capacity once demand rises. Standardized sample kits, defined shipping routes and a regulated incoming inspection ensure that quality remains the same even as volume grows — a reliability you can count on in everyday clinical work.
